# Tiny Chess Engine (`make.fun` Tiny Chess-01 Bounty)

A compact chess engine designed for the `make.fun` tiny-chess-01 bounty (strongest chess engine in at most 4 KB of Python).

- **Protocol**: Communicates through the `make.fun` env protocol.
- **Source**: `engine.py` contains the readable source code, and `main.py` is its LZMA-packed form generated by the kit's compress command.
- **Design**: Implements a 0x88 board representation with negamax alpha-beta search, quiescence search, transposition table, killer-move ordering, MVV-LVA capture ordering, and material + piece-square-table evaluation including castling, en passant, and a bishop-pair bonus.
- **Determinism**: Searches a fixed 50,000-node budget per move for fully deterministic gameplay.
